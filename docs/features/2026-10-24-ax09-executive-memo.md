## Delivered design

`analysis/investment_memo.py` defines an immutable presentation schema and separate research/portfolio adapters. `ui/investment_memo.py` and `analysis/memo_pdf.py` consume that same projection. Unified/classic saved research and advisor reports expose **Executive investment memo PDF**. Existing engines, reader PDF/memo/evidence formats and saved histories remain separate.

The executive page contains bounded saved thesis/allocation excerpts, evidence highlights, valuation, drivers, risks, quality/gaps and assumptions. Research numerical highlights reuse AX-04/06 audits at the latest valid saved retrieval reference. This establishes neither current freshness nor AI thesis verification; unsupported targets are never generated or promoted as verified figures. Figures retain currency, fiscal basis/period, source IDs/provider and full retrieval records in the appendix. Missing supported evidence is explicit. Portfolio first pages retain saved weights/cash, direct sector fractions, eligible historical volatility/drawdown with dates/benchmark/snapshot, descriptive roles and unverified ETF/factor exposures. Complete positions/roles, risk conventions and cash/risk assumptions follow.

PDFs retain full sanitized narrative/draft, both saved decision objects, detailed state and every evidence/reference record (no 100-record truncation). Portfolio daily series remain in the original complete evidence JSON. Raw objectives/prompts and credential-like keys are excluded; exact configured credentials and encoded forms are redacted; URLs lose queries/fragments; user-info/unsafe schemes are removed; nonfinite values become null; zero/negative values survive. Renderer markup is escaped. Vera glyphs render where available; unsupported characters become explicit Unicode codes instead of missing glyphs. Appendix wrapping preserves numerical tokens. Invariant canvas metadata makes unchanged exports byte-identical.

The appendix budget is two million characters. Oversized PDFs fail visibly without truncating saved state or removing original downloads. Session-local caching retains the latest sanitized memo/PDF for each renderer key and invalidates on changed saved content, including a later committee. There is no global report cache. Download uses `on_click="ignore"`; no collection, live refresh, new target or paid action is introduced.
