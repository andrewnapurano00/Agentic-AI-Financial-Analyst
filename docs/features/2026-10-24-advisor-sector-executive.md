# Sector-evidence Robo Advisor - 2026-10-24

The user requests a simpler Equity Report-like recommendation flow and explicitly confirms: metrics select, AI explains. The prior active Build allowed one structured AI response to reorder candidates and influence subjective risk budgets. The revised active Build and saved-evidence retry use explanation_only mode: local sector/mandate selection and sizing are fixed before AI. Only chosen holdings are sent, exact coverage is validated, and AI conviction/order cannot affect allocation. Unknown, missing or rejected holdings fail the explanation and preserve the local portfolio. Existing legacy selection mode remains available to retained engine consumers, and saved results are not silently recalculated.

The sector registry/priorities remain shared with Equity Report; no scores are imported from Equity Report. Current evidence is the existing narrower provider-TTM ratios plus separately dated annual revenue growth. This is not complete Equity Report metric coverage, independently audited four-quarter TTM, intrinsic valuation or future-performance certification. ETFs retain their fund-role/conditional-driver/risk framework; company ratios/scores are not applied to funds.

`portfolio_architect/executive.py` projects saved factors, score, usable coverage, main relative support/concern, actual metric values and provenance. No rescoring, collection or AI occurs in the projection. Provider fractions display as percentages and multiples as x; zero/negative values remain distinct. Unavailable legacy scores/coverage stay missing. Source dates, bases and IDs are available on demand. Existing summaries and narratives remain unchanged in saved packets.

`ui/advisor_executive.py` replaces repeated paragraphs for every holding with a recommendation table and one holding selector: why included, main risk, thesis-break condition and sector metrics. Deep audits, risk policy, advanced controls and exports remain behind existing expanders. New Builds label AI as explanatory; earlier AI-selected saved portfolios retain a legacy-workflow notice. Historical sizing labels no longer imply that explanatory AI controls covariance risk budgets. In the active workflow AI is optional and cannot resize holdings; cash and all hard caps remain local checks.

One explicit interpretation call and at most one explanation call remain the budget; no retries or model/provider calls on ordinary reruns, holding selection or downloads. Explanation input is the selected holdings rather than the broader candidate review pool (typically 10 selected vs up to 20 candidates; configured holding count can be 5-15). Byte/output caps and request timeout remain unchanged; no live latency/cost or model-quality improvement is claimed. Full S&P collection can still take several minutes. Existing session-only interruption recovery remains; durable restart recovery is not introduced.
