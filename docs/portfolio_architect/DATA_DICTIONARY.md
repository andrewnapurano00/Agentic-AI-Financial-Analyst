# Portfolio Architect data dictionary

All weights are fractions of total hypothetical equity; displays convert to percentages. Schemas reject nonfinite numbers and unsupported mandate fields. `architect-1.0` and `us-liquid-curated-20261019-v1` identify calculation/universe versions. Snapshot IDs hash the complete retained evidence including original retrieval timestamps. Result IDs hash snapshot, mandate, accepted weights and conviction. Model usage/prose are not financial evidence.

## Curated universe and eligibility

`portfolio_architect/universe.py` explicitly lists 20 stocks and 16 ETFs. This is a reproducible research starting list selected today, not a comprehensive screen or a historically frozen universe. No leveraged, inverse, crypto, options-income or single-stock funds are included. Current inclusion does not establish liquidity, listing or suitability: live eligibility requires a unique quote/profile, positive finite price, quote observation within seven UTC calendar days, explicit USD listing and no conflicting quote currency, matching provider ETF classification, supported US exchange and average-volume × quote-price turnover proxy of at least USD5 million/day. The average-volume window is unavailable. Missing stock sectors block eligibility. The provider is not independently audited for classification accuracy.

Stock sector labels come from the current FMP profile. ETF categories and theme tags are curated metadata, not measured fund exposures, issuer research or forecasts. Sector exclusions conservatively exclude **all ETFs** because look-through is unavailable. The sector cap applies to stock sectors and ETF categories; it does not enforce underlying combined equity sector exposure. Possible fund duplication is warned from documented category groups; no numerical constituent overlap or overlap ceiling is claimed. Theme preferences affect ranking, not guaranteed pure exposure. Risk preference does not establish suitability or impose a forecast volatility/loss target.

## Provider routes / fields

All routes use `https://financialmodelingprep.com/stable/` through existing `get_fmp_json`, with TLS verification, safe-GET retries, `(3,10)` quote/profile/ratio timeouts and `(3,15)` history timeout. Subscription access is unverified; errors return partial/missing data, never invented records. One quote batch plus profile/ratios/history for every unique requested name including benchmark; default36 names =109 primary logical dispatches before cache reuse. Missing batch rows permit at most one stable/quote fallback per symbol (maximum145 with the shipped36-name universe), concurrency three; HTTP401 and certificate failures do not fan out quote fallbacks. Concurrency is three for each company/history stage; retries may add HTTP attempts. Worst logical budget for the adapter's41-name validation ceiling is165 including individual quote fallback; the shipped universe permits36 unique names, including its benchmark.

## Ranking and construction

Local score starts at1, adds0.3 per curated preferred-theme match,0.2 for positive stock provider-TTM margin,0.4 for a curated defensive theme with conservative preference. These are disclosed descriptive heuristics, not fair value, sector-relative quality, probabilities or expected alpha. Selection first preserves sector-cap capacity, then priority. Requested holdings5–15, unique tickers, nonnegative weights, position/category caps, cash target and exact budget reconciliation are validated. Infeasible eligible-universe capacity blocks construction rather than renormalizing above caps. No trades or share counts are calculated.

Equal-weight signals are1. Risk sizing uses inverse daily volatility penalized by average positive pairwise correlation. Covariance uses sample (`ddof=1`) with20% diagonal shrinkage; zero/unstable variance or insufficient data falls back to capped equal weights. With validated AI conviction, risk signals are multiplied by0.8/1.0/1.2 for low/medium/high, a bounded subjective influence. Without accepted complete conviction, risk sizing remains the fallback. Proportional water-filling respects nested position/category caps; no optimizer or expected-return model is used. Hypothetical dollars use integer cents and largest-remainder allocation across holdings and cash, reconciling to the rounded account amount.

## Risk diagnostics

Require61 valid closes per selected holding and60 aligned simple daily returns. Common dates must cover at least90% of the union in the common span. All calculations use the intersection; omitted dates are disclosed and can produce multi-session returns, so annualization is approximate. There is no exchange-calendar completeness certification. Volatility = sample standard deviation of the weighted return series ×√252. Cash return and risk-free assumptions are zero; no Sharpe, alpha or beta is claimed. Correlations above0.85 are flagged; constant series correlation is undefined, not asserted zero. Drawdown includes initial wealth1 before the first return.

Portfolio cumulative price return/drawdown describe a **static daily-rebalanced mix of today's weights**, without fees/taxes/dividends, not performance of the prompt, manager or historical strategy. Benchmark requires every exact portfolio date and uses first-to-last price ratio; otherwise comparison is unavailable. All windows, sample counts, return basis and synthetic status are visible. No future volatility/return or historically point-in-time fundamental simulation is implied.

## AI and safe output contracts

Interpretation: one structured `InvestorMandate` with server-side feasibility validation; user edits before Build. Specialist review: one bounded call for four explicit responsibilities, then deterministic risk review/finalization. At most90 seconds, no SDK retry,42KB input,1800 interpretation /3800 specialist output-token caps per action; stage usage records attempts/tokens where available. Unknown pricing means no certified dollar ceiling. Separate repeated clicks are new paid attempts, not automatic retries.

`SpecialistOutput` permits eligible unique tickers, low/medium/high conviction, enum reason/concern/rejection codes and candidate-specific fact IDs. Quality requires a cited positive stock margin; theme/defensive requires tags. No free-form numerical model claim, executable code, tool call or hidden prompt is accepted/rendered. Company descriptions are not sent. Mechanical validation cannot certify model judgment or prove substantive independent deliberation. Failure preserves local allocations and successful evidence. Four responsibilities share one service call; they are not four independent agents.

JSON retains versioned mandate controls, evidence, histories, diagnostics, committee, usage and gaps; raw objective is omitted. CSV includes allocations, mode and snapshot metadata. HTML escapes all content and includes the saved report/provenance. Exact configured keys and recognized secret patterns are redacted; URLs are omitted; textual CSV formula prefixes are escaped. Downloads make no provider/model calls.
