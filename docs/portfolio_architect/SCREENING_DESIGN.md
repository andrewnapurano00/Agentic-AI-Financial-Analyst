# Advisor screening design — 2026-10-20

The advisor consumes current S&P 500 membership and provider TTM metrics through a focused provider module. It reuses the canonical Equity Report sector-applicability registry, not saved legacy Equity Report scorecards. Market context reuses `providers/market_dashboard.py`; metadata-only navigation does not carry financial evidence.

Membership compatibility correction: the stable membership endpoint may return HTTP 402 for a key that can read the legacy `/api/v3/sp500_constituent` route. The advisor makes one legacy compatibility attempt only on HTTP 402/403/404, retaining the actual route and sanitized warning. Both routes share the existing FMP transport, completeness/duplicate checks and screen cache. No fixed-list substitution or fallback on authentication, throttling or transport failure is introduced.

Rate-limit correction: a per-build `RequestBatch` paces logical provider requests in groups of one hundred, with a thirty-second interruptible cooldown and `(3,30)` connect/read timeouts. Its counter and stop event are shared across advisor collection stages; private cache arguments do not change credential-scoped evidence identities. Cached quote/history/screen rate-limit flags restore the stopped state. The shared dashboard reserves its quote-plus-instrument-history budget before running through its existing collector. Existing shared transport retries are unchanged and can add HTTP attempts beyond the logical-request count. Once a request reports HTTP429, queued work skips transport, history/issuer/macro stages are skipped, and successful earlier records remain available. Only already in-flight requests can finish. Quote payloads are also capped at one hundred symbols. No automatic fresh run or model retry is introduced.

`portfolio_architect/screening.py` owns eligibility, peer percentiles, the thirty-stock shortlist, dated macro interpretation and position sensitivities. Applicable metrics have equal fixed weights; ties use midranks, smaller positive valuation multiples and nonnegative leverage are favored. Missing metrics contribute zero rather than renormalizing the denominator. At least three sector observations per metric, three ranked metrics and 60% metric coverage are required. Positive provider TTM net margin and ROA are minimum gates. Financials omit generic industrial cash-flow/leverage measures. REITs and unknown sectors are excluded without appropriate evidence. This is a bounded provider-ratio screen, not full audited statement reconstruction or an assertion that specialist requirements are satisfied.

`providers/advisor_screen.py` collects all membership rows before narrowing. Bulk entitlement failures alone can use capped standard-endpoint fallback. Duplicate symbols are rejected. Two leaders per sector framework preserve alternatives before global sector-relative priority fills the remaining shortlist slots. Mandate exclusions precede shortlisting. `providers/advisor_research.py` independently validates four matched fiscal-year annual revenue records and retains up to three dated stock-news references. Annual growth modifies final descriptive priority by at most +0.3/-0.2 points; theme preference contributes 0.3 per matching tag. Sector screen contributes score/10, before legacy positive-margin and defensive preferences. Hard allocation capacity takes precedence over rank; selection is not simply the top ten scores.

Macro observations preserve original provider inputs and dates. Only observations within seven days and explicitly non-stale enter context. VIX 25 and the S&P 200-bar trend are disclosed descriptive heuristics. Yield-index moves remain relative index changes, not yield percentage-point changes. Macro commentary does not automatically change cash or allocation constraints. News titles are research leads, not verified catalyst impacts. No GDP/inflation/policy forecast is inferred from market proxies.

The immutable saved snapshot carries the screen inputs, component percentiles, peer counts, omissions, shortlist, macro packet, annual calculation inputs and news references. JSON/HTML exports retain this provenance through existing credential/URL scrubbing. Existing saved results remain readable and are never recollected on render. Optional AI receives a bounded subset including the feasible deterministic basket; mechanical validation and allocation caps still govern acceptance. Failed AI preserves local construction.

No new sidebar route or universal financial handoff is introduced. Trader engines and synthetic fixtures remain retained legacy source for tests; active advisor and standalone controls no longer expose them.


## 2026-10-21 design note: sector AI and covariance construction

R01/R04/R07/R08 scoped delivery. The original Equity Report registry remains authoritative for sector mapping and metric priorities; no legacy scorecard values are imported. Seventeen supported provider TTM fields include gross/EBITDA margins, P/S, P/FCF, dividend yield, debt/capital and debt-service coverage alongside the original profitability/value/leverage fields. These come from the same existing ratios requests, with fields checked against [FMP's TTM ratios documentation](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/metrics-ratios-ttm) and [bulk schema](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/ratios-ttm-bulk). Missing values never become zero. Higher/lower percentile directions are descriptive heuristics, not fair values or dividend-safety judgments. Required/preferred/other applicable metric weights are 2/1/0.5 with a fixed denominator and 60% weighted coverage requirement. Invalid nonpositive multiples are excluded, zero debt/yield remains valid, and banks exclude generic corporate debt/cash-flow ratios. No specialist inputs or estimates are fabricated; REITs remain excluded.

`analyst_packet.py` sends up to twenty active-screen alternatives, complete provenance on retained substantive facts, available per-metric sector percentiles, dated news, missing sector priorities and aligned historical volatility/correlation context. Optional facts/news have explicit omission counts; an unshrinkable packet fails locally before any model call. Packet budget remains42KB, structured review output cap is9000 tokens, timeout90 seconds, retries0, one review attempt on explicit Build. The interpretation action retains its separate1800-token cap. These are packet/output limits, not measured total prompt cost or dollar ceilings. AI is initially selected when OpenAI is configured; ordinary reruns remain call-free. Exact per-company references and framework names are validated; this is not semantic certification. Saved holding AI narratives and provenance remain separate from provider facts.

`construction.py` owns deterministic NumPy solvers, adding no provider/model calls or dependencies. Risk budgets solve the convex objective `0.5*x'C*x - sum(b_i*log(x_i))` by bounded cyclic coordinate descent, normalize to the noncash budget, then Euclidean-project onto positive position and disjoint sector/category constraints. AI budgets are proportional to0.8/1/1.2 conviction; without validated conviction they are equal. Projection can move achieved risk shares away from requested budgets, which the UI shows. Minimum variance uses projected gradient descent on the same covariance with cash/position/sector constraints built into each step. Both solvers use20% diagonal-shrunk sample covariance from at least60 aligned daily price returns and90% common-date coverage. Iterations are capped at2000 and nonconvergence/unstable history is disclosed as a capped equal-weight fallback. A1e-7 total-account holding floor retains the requested names; it is not a minimum executable trade. Final fractions reconcile within1e-9; dollar allocations retain cent reconciliation.

The saved allocation audit records the covariance window, risk contributions `w_i*(Cw)_i/(w'Cw)`, target budgets, sizing volatility, diversification ratio and effective holdings. Negative risk contributions indicate hedging. Risk shares exclude cash; effective holdings uses normalized invested weights. Historical mix diagnostics remain separately based on unshrunk sample returns. No forecasts, alpha, suitability target, outperformance claim, ETF look-through or covariance stability guarantee follows. Existing saved schemas load with empty new audit/thesis defaults; opening an older result never recalculates it. Other portfolio workspaces and their optimizers are unchanged.


## 2026-10-21 correction: growth intent and visible AI conviction

The prior implementation let quality/value dominate a tiny growth/theme bonus and selected new sector capacity before candidate rank. `mandate_fit.py` now makes explicit growth intent first-class. Growth stocks require saved positive annual revenue CAGR; missing/nonpositive values are ineligible. Growth/theme priority is a transparent heuristic: with themes,45% supported relevance,35% capped historical growth score and20% sector quality; without themes,65% growth and35% quality. Growth score reaches1 at30% annual CAGR; this is a saturation parameter, not a target/forecast. Provider descriptions mentioning AI/semiconductors/data centers score full thematic relevance; curated matches score half, with source limitations disclosed. Neither certifies AI revenue. A redundant growth theme is normalized out when style already equals growth. Original saved mandates default to balanced; the editable pending mandate infers growth from its prompt when the field is absent, while saved results never silently change.

Before annual follow-up exists, Growth/AI shortlisting reserves up to20 of30 candidates for the canonical technology/communication/industrial sector proxies from actual screened S&P membership, then retains quality alternatives. Proxies are a collection policy, not a recommendation or exposure fact. `optimizer.select` now follows rank subject to an exact future-capacity check over remaining holding slots, taking the largest concave per-sector capacity increments. It does not force new sectors when existing higher-ranked names allow feasible completion. Growth fund eligibility uses curated growth/AI/broad-equity metadata. No ticker blacklist, new stock universe, unbounded calls or new provider endpoint is introduced.

All AI review paths use at most20 alternatives including the feasible local basket. Growth evidence is protected during packet trimming and growth stock picks must cite both annual CAGR and an applicable sector metric. The structured analyst contract adds a bounded180-character conviction_summary, reused in the shared allocation table/CSV/HTML. Existing saved AI theses provide a bounded fallback summary; missing AI review stays explicitly unrated. `ai_review_status` records completed/not-requested/failed states with allowlisted diagnostics rather than raw model/provider errors. The allocation screen presents that status above the table, and legacy balanced results with a growth objective receive a rebuild notice. No AI quality or future growth certification follows from mechanical validation.


## 2026-10-21 correction: dense review packet recovery

A live screenshot established that the prior minimum-fact packet could still exceed42KB and never reach the model. `analyst_packet.py` now interns repeated exact source/basis/as_of/retrieved_at records in a provenance registry; facts keep their ID, field, value, unit and a provenance_id. No retained provenance text, dates or calculation formulas are truncated. The exact compact UTF-8 encoder is shared with structured_call, so preflight measures the submitted bytes. Optional macro/news/business context shrinks first, followed by nonessential metrics. If the minimum packet is still too large, lower-ranked optional alternatives may be omitted while every ticker in the feasible local basket remains protected. Protected growth citations and substantive sector metrics remain; removed provenance is pruned and omissions are explicit. Irreducible oversized mandatory evidence still fails safely before a model call.

Response validation permits only tickers and exact fact IDs actually submitted, including after candidate omission. No provider refresh or model retry is automatic. The explicit Retry AI review using saved evidence action reuses the saved snapshot and retains prior usage records, tagging the new review action. Existing eligibility/freshness requirements are checked before submission; stale evidence requires explicit refresh/Build and leaves the prior result available. New known packet-budget failures record zero model attempts. No source or universe expansion, token-limit increase, paid live check or financial recalculation on ordinary reruns is introduced.
