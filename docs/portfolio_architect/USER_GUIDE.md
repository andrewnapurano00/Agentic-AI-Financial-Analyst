# Use AI Portfolio Architect

1. Open **AI Robo Advisor**. Configure FMP in Settings. Portfolio construction opens directly; trader and synthetic-demo controls have been removed.
2. Enter an **Investment objective**, for example “Build a diversified five-year quality-growth portfolio.” Click **Interpret mandate**. Review all assumptions; local interpretation extracts only supported explicit risk, horizon and theme preferences.
3. Review **Investment style**, **Horizon**, **Risk preference**, **Hypothetical amount**, **Holdings**, **Allowed assets** and **Sizing method**. Open **Allocation limits, exclusions and themes** for cash, position and sector/category caps, exclusions and benchmark.
4. Click **Build portfolio**. Stock candidates come from current S&P 500 membership. Quality requires positive provider TTM net margin and ROA, at least 60% of sector-priority weighted metric coverage and three usable sector peer ranks. Quality, cash flow, valuation and leverage use the Equity Report sector-applicability registry. Applicable must-have metrics receive weight 2, preferred metrics 1, and other applicable metrics 0.5; missing data retains its denominator weight. Missing metrics reduce the score. REITs remain excluded without FFO/AFFO evidence. For balanced style, the shortlist preserves two leaders per sector framework, then fills to thirty by score. Growth/AI reserves research capacity for up to twenty technology, communication-services and industrial candidates plus quality alternatives; this sector proxy is not verified AI exposure. After issuer research, growth-style stocks require positive comparable three-year annual revenue CAGR. Supported business-description/curated theme matches and historical growth lead the final ranking; a high value score alone cannot displace them. Selection follows ranking whenever the remaining holding slots can still satisfy cash and sector/position caps. Growth style limits eligible ETFs to curated growth/AI or broad-equity classifications; fund look-through remains unavailable.
5. Read **Allocation**, **Holding research**, **Risk & tradeoffs** and **Investment committee**. Each stock has supporting metrics, available issuer description, comparable annual revenue growth, recent dated news, counterarguments and sector/market sensitivities. News headlines are developments to investigate, not verified investment catalysts. Forward estimates, price targets and company-specific catalyst impacts remain unverified.
6. Open **Evidence & exports** for the screening audit, original facts, metric coverage, exclusion reasons and Market Brief market indicators. VIX, market trend, rates, dollar and oil observations provide descriptive risk/tailwind context. They do not predict returns or override your cash target. Stale, future or undated macro observations are omitted. Unavailable macro evidence never implies a benign regime.
7. Download CSV, JSON or HTML, or **Save session snapshot**. JSON/HTML retain screening and position evidence but remove raw objectives and external URLs. News source links remain available in the app. Downloads and saved reports do not collect data or call AI. Download important work before closing the session.

## Collection and recovery

If the stable S&P membership endpoint is restricted or unavailable (HTTP 402/403/404), Build tries the legacy FMP membership endpoint once. The saved screening audit records the actual source and warning. Both endpoints must still provide a complete constituent list; authentication, rate-limit and connection failures do not trigger this fallback. After a failure, enable **Refresh provider evidence on next Build** and click **Build portfolio** to retry.

Screening checks all current members rather than a small fixed stock list. Bulk access uses three screen requests; plan-restricted bulk metrics fall back to at most 1,100 standard metric requests at concurrency three. Screening lasts one hour in cache. A full screen may take several minutes. Authentication, rate-limit and transient bulk failures do not trigger that fallback. Provider subscription coverage is required; missing data can block construction rather than fabricate a portfolio.

Advisor collection now shares a limit of **100 provider requests per batch**, followed by a **30-second pause** before the next batch. Requests use a **30-second read timeout** and a three-second connection timeout. Quote requests contain at most one hundred tickers. Screening, quote/profile/ratio, history and issuer-research stages share the same counter; the bounded Market Brief collection reserves room before running. Concurrency remains three. Shared transport retries can add HTTP attempts and time, so this is not a thirty-second deadline for the entire build. The standard per-company screen for roughly five hundred members can include about five minutes of deliberate pauses, plus provider and follow-up research time.

If FMP returns **HTTP 429**, the run stops scheduling further collection; up to three already in-flight requests may finish. It keeps successful collected data and previous reports and shows a rate-limit recovery message. It does not switch to individual quote requests after a rate-limited batch. Wait for the provider limit to reset before retrying. Ordinary reruns do not restart collection; use **Refresh provider evidence on next Build** and **Build portfolio** for an explicit retry. Pausing cannot bypass a daily quota or an endpoint entitlement restriction.

Only up to thirty shortlisted stocks plus sixteen curated ETFs receive quote, profile, ratio and history research. Up to sixty additional requests collect four annual revenues and recent news for stocks; these cache for one hour. Market evidence reuses the Market Brief dashboard provider and its five-minute cache. Quotes cache for five minutes and histories for six hours. Shared safe retries can add HTTP attempts. All collection requires **Build portfolio**. **Refresh provider evidence on next Build** clears the relevant caches on that explicit action.

Use **Latest collection diagnostics** for partial provider failures and exclusions. If there are too few eligible companies or insufficient sector capacity, check evidence access first, then revise constraints as appropriate. Previous reports remain available on failure. Old reports remain readable with their original evidence; they do not acquire new screening research automatically.

**Use bounded AI interpretation and specialist review** is selected initially when OpenAI is configured; you can turn it off for local construction. Use a model supporting structured outputs. Interpret and Build each permit one model attempt, without automatic retries. The review considers up to twenty researched alternatives, including the feasible local basket, plus bounded macro context. A refused, incomplete or invalid review preserves the deterministic portfolio. The AI supplies a cited investment thesis, sector risks, missing inputs, portfolio role and thesis-break condition for each proposed holding. Read these in **Holding research**. Citations and framework membership are checked; prose semantics remain unverified. Conviction is subjective, not a return probability.

## Choose how positions are weighted

1. Select **AI conviction + covariance risk budgets** to let validated AI conviction influence target risk shares. Low/medium/high conviction correspond to relative budgets of 0.8/1.0/1.2. With AI off or invalid, equal risk budgets apply.
2. Select **Equal covariance risk budgets** to balance historical risk contributions before applying caps, or **Constrained minimum variance** to minimize historical covariance risk within your cash, position and sector limits. These methods use correlations across holdings, not just individual volatility. **Equal weight** and the two labeled legacy heuristics remain available.
3. Click **Build portfolio** to apply a changed method. Review **Allocation and risk contribution**: capital weights, target risk budgets and achieved risk shares can differ. Caps are applied to risk-budget portfolios after the initial solve, so exact risk parity is not promised. Cash is reserved first. Risk-budget targets are not return estimates.
4. Inspect effective holdings (concentration), diversification ratio and sizing volatility, alongside the dated historical mix diagnostics in **Risk & tradeoffs**. Sizing uses 20% diagonal-shrunk sample covariance; historical mix volatility uses unshrunk sample returns. Both annualize at 252 sessions and assume zero cash return/risk-free rate. At least 60 aligned returns and 90% common-date coverage are required. Missing history, unstable variance or a failed solver causes a disclosed equal-weight fallback subject to caps.

## Interpret the tradeoffs

Scores measure available provider TTM ratios within the current sector population. Fiscal end dates may be unavailable; these are not independently reconstructed audited TTM metrics or the full Equity Report scorecard. Annual revenue CAGR requires four consecutive matched fiscal years and consistent reported currency; it is not a TTM or forward growth estimate. Sparse evidence reduces confidence. A top screen rank cannot establish intrinsic value, management quality or future outperformance.

ETFs remain a curated list of broad, sector, bond and diversifying funds; stock ratios do not rank funds. Fund fees, tracking, constituent overlap and underlying sector exposures are not measured. Any excluded stock sector conservatively excludes all ETFs. Category caps do not certify underlying exposure.

Historical mix diagnostics use current securities, aligned daily prices, no missing-price filling and zero cash return. FMP adjustment is unverified and dividend cash flows are absent. This is not a point-in-time strategy backtest. Current membership introduces survivor bias. Risk preference does not enforce a future loss ceiling. No broker connection, scheduled monitoring or automatic rebalance is provided.

Large AI Robo Advisor evidence downloads use **Evidence JSON (.gz)** when needed. Extract the file to read the complete JSON; all saved evidence is retained. If compressed evidence still exceeds the download limit, the report, Allocation CSV and Research HTML remain available. Downloads reuse saved results.

### Conviction explanations and review status

Read **Conviction / AI rationale** in Allocation for the saved rating and brief company-specific AI explanation. The status above the tabs distinguishes completed AI review, local-only selection and a failed AI review with sanitized diagnostics. A missing review never receives an invented AI summary. An older saved AI thesis can supply the brief explanation without another call; older local results stay unrated. For a growth objective, choose **Investment style ? Growth**, review **Preferred curated themes**, then explicitly **Build portfolio**. Existing saved reports remain unchanged until you build again. The stock universe remains current S&P 500 constituents and the ETF universe remains curated; no individual ticker exclusions were added by this correction.

If a saved Robo Advisor result says AI review was unavailable, click **Retry AI review using saved evidence** when OpenAI is configured. This explicit action reuses collected company evidence without repeating provider collection. If the saved quotes are too old, the app keeps your result and asks you to refresh and Build instead. Downloads and ordinary reruns never retry AI automatically.
