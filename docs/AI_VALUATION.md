# AI Valuation: automatic company price scenarios

The active workspace integrates the supplied shell additively. Existing app configuration, sidebar and typed company handoffs remain authoritative. The earlier manual compatibility/Apply/Calculate sequence is superseded by one explicit **Generate AI Valuation** action.

## Evidence and assumptions

FMP requests include quote/profile; up to5annual income/balance/cash-flow rows; up to6annual consensus rows; provider TTM ratios/key metrics; analyst-target summary; and up to400daily historical rows. Annual actuals, provider TTM facts, forward forecasts, technical observation dates and analyst publication windows stay distinct. Metric packets reuse existing app formulas and retain units, currency, fiscal/as-of dates, source IDs and retrieval dates. No saved Deep Research financial packet is silently trusted. News, peer medians and macro forecasts are not invented.

Both `epsAvg`/`numAnalystsEps` and legacy `estimatedEpsAvg`/`numberAnalystEstimatedEps` aliases are accepted. Positive finite annual consensus EPS requires positive integer analyst coverage, a future fiscal date compatible with the issuer's annual year-end, and no explicit currency conflict. Missing estimate currency uses a disclosed same-issuer annual-reporting-currency assumption. Missing direct quote currency may use profile-reported listing currency, with corporate-action/scaling compatibility disclosed as a scenario assumption. Missing peers or macro lowers confidence rather than blocking eligible conditional cases. These assumptions are not independent verification of units or source accuracy.

Known quote/profile/selected-statement FX conflicts, missing listing/reporting currency, stale/future quote/retrieval evidence and unusable positive denominators still block targets. Quotes max7UTC calendar days; annual basis550days; required source retrieval24hours. All sectors, including unknown classifications, have a conditional earnings method in the production disclosed-assumptions workflow. REITs use provisional earnings P/E with explicit depreciation/property-gain and missing FFO/AFFO/NAV limitations. Materials and Energy disclose unnormalized cycle earnings; Utilities disclose missing rate-base/regulatory/dividend models. Unknown classifications use company evidence without invented sector averages.

Financials prefer year-end common equity/common shares when dated compatible evidence supports it. Missing preferred stock is not zero; diluted weighted-average shares never substitute for year-end common shares. If the common-book contract is unavailable, nonpositive, stale or currency-incompatible, the workflow independently attempts an earnings P/E fallback using annual income evidence. This does not validate or repair rejected book inputs. Strict domain callers without disclosed assumptions retain their prior sector/book restrictions.

The earnings method prefers provider-reported annual diluted EPS and positive annual diluted weighted-average shares. If EPS is absent, a disclosed historical proxy can use positive net income divided by positive diluted weighted-average shares from the same annual record. This proxy is not reported diluted EPS or verified earnings attributable to common shareholders. Its source, formula and label are retained; history growth does not mix proxy and reported EPS, and consensus EPS stays context only rather than replacing the proxy anchor. Explicit zero/negative/invalid EPS is not replaced with a positive proxy. Missing or nonpositive net income/share inputs cannot create a proxy. Missing annual data, invalid quote/units or incompatible currencies still prevent a numerical target; profitable TTM/forward-only turnarounds without a usable annual basis remain outside this annual engine.


## Cases and numbers

AI selects independent growth and exit multiples for Bear/Base/Bull, justified by historical growth, profitability/cash generation, valuation, annual forecasts, technical momentum and analyst target context. New generations use `dated-consensus-v1`: the exit date is exactly 3/4/5 calendar years after the saved quote date (February 29 rolls to February 28 where necessary). For P/E, the engine anchors EPS to the nearest supported forward annual consensus within 550 days of the quote, with at least three analysts and compatible currency. Duplicate forecast dates are not used as an anchor. Growth then applies only over the fractional remaining years, using actual days / 365.25. All three scenarios share this near-term consensus bridge; they do not separately model its uncertainty. Missing/sparse/distant forecasts, earnings-proxy cases and P/B use the annual basis and grow from its fiscal date to the exit date. Normalized forecast currency may still be assumed as disclosed; GAAP/adjusted EPS compatibility and corporate-action basis are not independently verified.

Target = anchor EPS/common BVPS * (1 + case growth)^remaining growth years * exit multiple. Signed price return = target/reference quote - 1; annualized price return = (target/reference)^(1/selected horizon) - 1. Each card shows projected exit EPS/BVPS, multiple, anchor value/date, remaining growth years and exit date. The model receives exact projection bases plus forecast-to-forecast growth guides; growth means growth AFTER the anchor, avoiding double counting the consensus uplift. Technicals cannot independently justify earnings or multiples. All case prices remain ordered at supported horizons, with no forced relationship to today's quote. Legacy saved targets retain their original annual-basis/exact-horizon arithmetic and require explicit Generate to upgrade.

These are conditional nominal future exit prices, not present fair value, probability-weighted forecasts or guaranteed prices. No dividends, dilution, corporate actions, FX or leverage projections are included. Analysts' month/quarter publication windows are context rather than the3/4/5-year target horizon. Base growth/multiple fields must match the Base scenario. Case assumptions may be edited after generation and recalculated without AI. Horizon changes locally reuse the saved annual driver assumptions, not a freshly generated opinion.

## Controls, cost and recovery

Generate explicitly loads missing/enrichment evidence and runs one model request. Refresh metrics performs collection without AI. Ordinary page/ticker/horizon/formatting changes do not run models or collect new evidence. Five-feed legacy shell evidence upgrades only on explicit Generate. Old saved views remain readable; no old report is silently rewritten or financial value recalculated.

90-second model timeout, zero retries,9000completion-token cap,36k-character packet ceiling,500-word prompt target/600-word hard prose ceiling including three short rationales. Pydantic/schema, exact ticker/fingerprint, allowed/relevant source IDs, finite driver bounds, Base consistency and ordered targets are deterministic checks. They cannot certify semantics or investment correctness. Source references stay in the audit; executive references and empty citation punctuation are removed.

Eight-entry ticker/credential-scoped saved evidence, views, scenarios and controls remain. Refresh failures retain independently successful components with original timestamps; invalid generation retains the prior valid view/targets. Safe allowlisted failure status persists until explicit success; no SDK body or arbitrary exception text is displayed. See PLAN D/V-20261018-AI-PRICE-CASES for current verification; previous recovery records describe the superseded manual gate.

Edited cases are labeled User-edited; the saved AI view is retained as its original opinion and is explicitly not re-reviewed automatically. Generate refreshes metrics older than five minutes only on that explicit action; ordinary navigation never upgrades or refreshes evidence.

## Metric reference contract and input visibility

`valuation/citations.py` validates semantic support categories and recursively resolvable derived source IDs rather than requiring raw-row prefixes. Numeric growth assumptions can use actual annual financials, supplied growth/profitability metrics or compatible annual forecasts. Multiples can use supplied valuation metrics, ratios, quotes, forecast or analyst-target context. A technical-only citation cannot establish financial scenario drivers. The per-request JSON schema enumerates only actual role-appropriate IDs; local unknown-ID/support checks still run. Dangling/cyclic metric provenance is rejected. Safe diagnostics identify the failed field/reason and recognized IDs/counts, never arbitrary model/SDK content. This is mechanical validation of references and input type, not proof of economic semantics or forecast accuracy.

`valuation/coverage.py` builds What the AI is using from the actual packet with familiar metric names, correctly scaled percentages/ratios, separate quote/reporting currency, basis and period/observation dates. Counts are included-input counts, not a guarantee every metric influenced the opinion. This workspace shares company metric/sector/technical logic with the broader app but does not include Equity Report's full quarterly or peer-set analysis.

Forecast anchor freshness: normalized estimates must have a non-future retrieval timestamp no older than24hours at evidence preparation. Older or undated retained estimates remain context but cannot anchor new targets.


## Explicit growth units and assumption review

Version5 packets specify growth_pct in numeric percentage units:20.0 means20%, whereas0.20 means0.20%. Every newly generated scenario also requires annual_growth_decimal (0.20 for20%). Deterministic validation checks decimal == growth_pct/100; mismatches fail with an allowlisted growth-units message and preserve prior results. Neither value is silently multiplied by100 based on a guess. Genuine near-zero growth remains valid when consistently represented. Saved legacy cases remain readable without automatic migration or calls; manual edits synchronize both representations.

The screenshot's0.1/0.2/0.3% assumptions are consistent with a possible fraction-versus-percentage error, but the original response's intended units are unknown. Schema descriptions and examples now remove that ambiguity at generation time. Growth and valuation drivers compares actual Base growth, historical annual per-share growth, supported forecast-to-forecast growth and price/anchor earnings alongside the original AI growth/multiple rationales. All three scenarios within1% with a supported consensus CAGR >=5% trigger an explanatory review flag, not a rescaling/rejection rule. Comparisons keep fiscal dates and horizon distinctions explicit; duplicate forecast dates are excluded. AI must explain materially different growth and exit-multiple assumptions; that semantic quality is not certified by mechanical validation.

What would it take for the stock to double? reverses the exact dated target formula at each displayed exit multiple: required exit earnings = 2*reference price/multiple; required growth = (required earnings/anchor earnings)^(1/remaining growth years)-1. It is a hypothetical thesis check, not another recommendation, minimum target or guaranteed return. It uses the saved quote/anchor/currency and no provider/model call. Growth needed for a target may exceed allowed scenario-edit bounds, so this table does not automatically apply it. For the screenshot's rounded values (214.13 price,9.28 forecast EPS,22x multiple), doubling requires about17% annual post-anchor EPS growth; 0.2% and20% are materially different assumptions. These calculations do not verify issuer forecasts, future prices or GAAP/adjusted EPS compatibility.

## Equity Report reuse and common-BVPS repair (October18)

AI Valuation previously loaded its own restricted feed packet. Equity Report's saved scorecard is a derived view with mixed TTM, annual and forward measures; it does not establish an annual common-share denominator. The standardized FMP balance-sheet schema does not include the year-end outstanding-share field the valuation gate required. The as-reported schema includes XBRL common-share tags. Primary contracts checked: [standardized balance sheet](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/balance-sheet-statement), [as-reported balance sheet](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/as-reported-balance-statements), and [FMP formulas](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/formula). Provider-reported TTM book value is context, not proof of annual common BVPS.

### Design note: scoped raw-evidence reuse, not a scorecard bridge

`providers/company_evidence.py` captures allowlisted raw FMP responses during a new explicit active Equity Report run. It saves at most eight company/credential-scoped records in the current Streamlit session. Symbols, provider identity, request path, period and original response retrieval time stay attached. No prompts, ratings, downloads, full session state or credentials are transferred. Legacy scorecards/generated-at labels are not retroactively treated as provider evidence. Separate sessions, tickers and credential scopes do not share this session store. Deep Research's unverified saved-financial-packet bridge remains disabled.

Generate can reuse compatible quote/profile, provider ratios/key metrics, estimates and whole historical series; quarterly income/cash-flow/balance inputs remain separate. Missing annual evidence is collected independently. Cached request reuse limits are five minutes for quotes/ratios/key metrics, one hour for estimates/history and24hours for statement/profile records; every reused component retains its original timestamp. Source validation and financial eligibility still run. Refresh company metrics deliberately bypasses session reuse to make new provider requests, without a model call. Ordinary reruns/ticker/horizon/format changes do neither. A new Equity Report run is needed to populate the new store; older completed Equity Reports remain readable and are not regenerated automatically.

`valuation/metrics.py` feeds compatible saved quarterly income/cash-flow rows through canonical company-terminal TTM validation/calculations. Derived citations identify the actual quarter inputs, including both income and cash flow for FCF margin. Annual target anchors, calculated TTM, provider TTM and forward estimates remain distinct. Provider BVPS aliases formerly discarded by the allowlist are available as explicitly unverified-denominator context; they do not enable common-book targets. This remains a bounded input subset, not every Equity Report specialist/peer calculation.

### Year-end common shares

For financials missing standardized common shares, `providers/valuation.py` makes one additional bounded annual as-reported request (up to5rows, same transport, timeouts and3-worker ceiling; optional source recovery is sequential). `valuation/book_value.py` matches issuer, FY period, fiscal date/year, reporting currency and available publication dates. It accepts `commonstocksharesoutstanding` or explicitly reported issued common shares minus treasury common shares. Missing treasury shares are not zero; issued/authorized shares alone, common-stock currency amounts and diluted weighted-average shares cannot fill the denominator. Duplicate/contradictory, future, currency/year-mismatched and stale sources cannot enable targets. The existing common-equity rule still requires direct common equity or total stockholders' equity minus explicit preferred stock.

Recorded FMP annual as-reported dates can differ from normalized dates by one day. That narrow exception additionally requires the same explicit fiscal year and independently matching total stockholders' equity; original dates and the reconciliation note remain in the audit. No broader date tolerance or silent quarter/TTM substitution is used. The recorded JPM fixture independently checks `(362438000000-20045000000)/(4104933895-1408700000)`; it is test evidence, not a current investment value.

The UI displays reused components and original timestamps, a computed annual common-BVPS metric with source references, provider BVPS as context only, and denominator guidance when eligibility still fails. Saved AI views/targets remain in place with mismatch warnings after metric changes. Evidence version3 upgrades only on explicit Generate/Refresh. REIT FFO/AFFO, commodity normalization, full bank specialist KPIs, peer medians and macro forecasts are not fabricated or unlocked by this repair. No model is called when the financial basis remains ineligible.

Energy scenarios disclose commodity-price, production, refining-margin and capital-spending sensitivity in the page and model evidence. No commodity-price deck, reserves/NAV model or synthetic normalized EPS is fabricated. The existing positive-earnings, currency, identity and freshness gates still apply. Generate remains the explicit model action; changing horizons reuses saved cases.

October18 all-sector update supersedes earlier sector-wide exclusions: the conditional production workflow now has provisional earnings coverage for REITs, Materials and unclassified issuers, plus financial P/E fallback and a disclosed net-income-per-diluted-share proxy. No specialist FFO, normalized earnings or NAV values are fabricated.
