# Agent Trading Lab design note — 2026-10-18

This experimental third Portfolio Lab tab adapts the four fictional personas from the user's local course example without importing its services or scheduler. The reference remains untouched and ignored by Git and Docker. The existing optimizer and portfolio manager retain their own workflows.

## Boundaries

`paper_trading/engine.py` owns account arithmetic and market/constraint validation; `agent.py` owns bounded structured proposals; `workflow.py` owns explicit rounds, checkpoints and recovery. `providers/paper_market.py` owns FMP transport and a five-minute cache. `ui/paper_trading_tab.py` renders session-backed accounts and exports. Demo is fully synthetic and offline. FMP + AI uses the configured shared OpenAI client with a 90-second timeout and no model retry; four independent accounts mean at most four model attempts per fresh round. Failed attempts count even when usage is unavailable.

One shared market snapshot uses the existing TLS-verified FMP transport: stable batch-quote, profile and ratios-ttm. Eight universe symbols plus a separate benchmark require at most eighteen logical HTTP dispatches; bounded safe transport retries can add attempts. Profile and ratio failures retain valid quotes. See [FMP batch quote documentation](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/batch-quote). Quotes must be explicitly USD or supported by an explicit USD listing profile; currency conflicts block use. Profile-only currency is disclosed, rather than proof of quote units. No FX or provider stitching occurs.

## Financial and recovery contracts

Snapshots expire fifteen minutes after retrieval; quote observations older than seven UTC calendar days or future observations are rejected. Identity hashes exclude retrieval time, so refreshing identical evidence cannot authorize a duplicate completed round. A missing held quote blocks account valuation instead of assigning zero or using cost basis.

Models propose complete long-only target weights or HOLD; deterministic validation checks persona, experiment, snapshot, available symbols, cash reserve and position caps. Cathie accepts only IBIT/BITO. Rebalancing solves post-cost equity against proportional turnover fees, then sells before buying fractional shares. Orders are atomic per account. Paper reference-price execution has no liquidity, slippage, dividends, taxes or actual broker fills. Subsequent price drift is not automatically rebalanced.

Each persona is checkpointed independently before and after a model attempt. Successful accounts survive another agent's failure or interruption. Retries process failed accounts only. All-success repetition is a no-op. Failure status is distinct from a deliberate HOLD. Observed drawdown retains its peak across bounded chart-history truncation. Benchmark returns require an initial baseline and same-calendar-date held quotes; this is not exact intraday alignment or total return.

## Scope and verification

### Historical replay extension

`paper_trading/replay.py` and `ui/paper_replay_tab.py` add an independent dated workflow. User-defined periods are completed dates of at most366 days, with daily/five-session/21-session decisions and at most24 decision dates. `providers/paper_history.py` uses shared FMP transport and at most two logical requests per symbol (nine including a separate benchmark), concurrency three and a six-hour history cache; safe HTTP retries can add attempts. Current profiles supply only a USD listing eligibility check and never enter agent evidence. Historical quote-unit compatibility remains an assumption. [FMP's historical-price guide](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/how-to/fmp-historical-price-apis-from-light-charts-to-dividendadjusted-analysis) documents the full EOD endpoint. Replay uses its close series, split adjusted, excluding dividend cash flows; retrospective corrections/split adjustment can reflect information unavailable at the historical date.

Normalize prices and freeze the successful packet. Use a complete common observed calendar; no missing-bar interpolation or stale-price substitution. Show actual common coverage, which can be narrower than requested. Reject internal missing sessions and fewer than two common dates. Historical AI receives only allowlisted prices through the decision date, trailing12 closes and calculated20-session indicators, plus its own past account history. Future series, endpoint dates, schedules, current fundamentals, news and other workspace packets are not passed to the model. The full bounded series remains in local replay state and export for simulation execution, never a model tool. A current model can still remember later events; filtering supplied evidence is not proof of training-time blindness or a genuine point-in-time database.

All four proposals are collected before executing any at the following common session close. Weight sizing and proportional fees use that next close, with existing validated post-fee cash/cap arithmetic. No same-observation close fills. Initial benchmark purchase is the first common close; trader accounts hold cash until their first next-close execution. Daily marks, including the final date after the last decision, use the same calendar and price-return basis. Historical daily history is retained in full within the one-year bound. Date labels denote sessions, not verified exchange-local closing instants.

Per-persona attempt/proposal/fill checkpoints allow safe resumption: a started/interrupted or failed call is never automatically retried or paid twice; an already-completed fill is skipped. Failure retains holdings and is distinct from HOLD in the decision log. A complete replay cannot advance again. Each explicit next-decision click attempts at most four models with existing bounded clients; navigation/downloads do none. Six session-backed historical experiments are separate from six live/demo experiments. There is no scheduler, broker, persistent restore, historical filings/news, delisted-universe reconstruction, liquidity/slippage/tax/dividend model or claim of predictive investment skill.

Maximum six experiments and fifty snapshots/rounds per experiment bound the session shell. Manual rounds do not run after closing the app. JSON/CSV exports retain paper flags and dated evidence, with credential filtering; there is no account import or restart persistence. Four styles do not establish actual investor behavior, macro analysis, calculated risk parity or profitable AI predictions. Tests use synthetic responses and block external transport. PLAN.md records executed verification separately from these design contracts.
